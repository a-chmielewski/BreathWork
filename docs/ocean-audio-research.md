# Ocean field-recording research

Research date: 2026-09-07

## Recommendation

Use **“Ocean waves” by orlandorizo** as the primary source. It is a genuine on-location recording, carries CC0 1.0, has the smallest original download of the two finalists, and its calm Pacific-beach character is the closer fit for breathwork. Use **“Waves Northsea” by sphion** as the fallback when a longer stereo recording is more important than source size.

Both originals require a free Freesound account to download. Freesound states that signed-in users receive the uploader's original file without editing or transcoding ([download FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/#how-do-i-download-a-sound), [preview/original FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/#i-hear-a-high-pitched-squeaking-in-the-audio-preview-what-is-that)). After acquisition, CC0 permits an original or derivative file to be committed to a public repository and distributed in the PWA.

The current project allows one optional ambient asset up to **4.5 MB** and currently bundles a 4,124,424-byte procedural AAC file ([performance budget](./performance-budget.md), [audio asset record](./audio-assets.md)). Any replacement should remain within that budget after AAC transcoding. No candidate was auditioned during this research, so listening and an acoustic seam check remain necessary before choosing loop boundaries.

## Best candidate: “Ocean waves”

- **Exact source:** [https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/588366/](https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/588366/)
- **Creator/uploader:** `orlandorizo`.
- **Real field recording:** Yes. The uploader says it was recorded at Tupilapa Beach on Nicaragua's Pacific coast with a Zoom H5 and Rode VideoMic Pro; this is direct capture provenance, not a synthesized sound.
- **Original media:** WAV, 1:37.040, approximately 13.3 MB, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono.
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication. The sound page links the work to CC0; the primary license text is [https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en).
- **Download accessibility:** The page exposes the original-file endpoint at [https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/588366/download/588366__orlandorizo__ocean-waves.wav](https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/588366/download/588366__orlandorizo__ocean-waves.wav), which redirects unauthenticated visitors to Freesound login. A registered account is required.
- **Attribution:** Not required by CC0. A voluntary provenance credit to `orlandorizo` and the source page is still advisable.
- **Editing, transcoding, looping, and redistribution:** Allowed, including commercial use. CC0 waives the affirmer's copyright and related rights and supplies an irrevocable public-license fallback where the waiver is ineffective; its stated scope includes reproduction, adaptation, and distribution in any medium and for any purpose ([CC0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en), [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).
- **Repository fit:** Best of the finalists. The mono original is 19.4 MB smaller than the fallback and should transcode efficiently for an offline ambient layer. Legal permission to loop is clear, but seamless loop points were not assessed without downloading or auditioning the recording.

All recording and file facts above come from the [uploader's Freesound source page](https://freesound.org/people/orlandorizo/sounds/588366/).

## Account-free alternative: “Sea Waves”

- **Exact source:** [https://bigsoundbank.com/sea-waves-s0698.html](https://bigsoundbank.com/sea-waves-s0698.html)
- **Creator:** Joseph SARDIN.
- **Real field recording:** Yes. The source page identifies it as waves recorded at the Atlantic Ocean between Soulac-sur-Mer and Lacanau, France, using a Tascam DR-40.
- **Original media:** 2:46.8, 48 kHz, 16-bit field recording. The page describes stereo XY capture. Direct probes found a stereo 320 kbit/s MP3 and a mono Ogg Vorbis download, so the MP3 is the safer available source unless the WAV obtained through the page's download form is verified.
- **License:** CC0/public-domain equivalent. The source page explicitly permits editing, processing, remixing, commercial use, and redistribution without permission.
- **Download accessibility:** No account is required. The [MP3](https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/0698.mp3) and [Ogg](https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/ogg/0698.ogg) endpoints returned successful audio responses during this review.
- **Attribution:** Not required; credit to Joseph SARDIN and BigSoundBank is still advisable as provenance.
- **Repository fit:** Immediately obtainable without credentials and long enough for a less repetitive loop. It must still be auditioned for comfort, checked for a clean loop seam, and transcoded below the 4.5 MB AAC budget.

## License screen and rejected material

CC0 is suitable for a raw public-repository asset because it permits copying, modification, and redistribution without attribution conditions. Its limitations still matter: CC0 does not waive third-party patent, trademark, privacy, or publicity rights and provides no warranty ([CC0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en)). These ocean-only recordings do not identify speech, performers, or third-party music on their source pages, reducing those practical concerns without eliminating the need to audition the selected file.

Recordings labeled only with **Public Domain Mark 1.0** were rejected. The mark is not a license grant, and its own primary text warns that a marked work may not be free of copyright restrictions in every jurisdiction ([Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)). For example, the otherwise relevant [“Ocean waves, Cox's Bazar beach” by Sukanta Majumdar](https://archive.org/details/aporee_55131_63018) is labeled only with that mark, so it creates avoidable uncertainty for a source-controlled asset.

CC BY 4.0 recordings can legally be adapted and redistributed, but they require credit, a license link, and an indication of changes ([CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). They were not advanced because the two CC0 finalists remove those ongoing compliance obligations.
